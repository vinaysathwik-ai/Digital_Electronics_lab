Virtual Digital Electronics Lab Guide

1. Wiring Patch Cords

• Click/drag from any terminal (Switch, IC pin, LED, or Vcc/GND rail) towards another terminal. As you get close, the wire magnetically snaps to the target port!

• Click-to-Connect: You can also simply click terminal A to start a cord, then click terminal B to complete it.

2. Powering Your ICs (SRM Lab Manual Rule 5)

• Every integrated circuit must have power connected: Pin 14 (or 16) wired to the +5V (Vcc) rail, and Pin 7 (or 8) wired to the GND rail.

3. Interactive Switches & LEDs

• Click the rocker switches (SW0–SW7) on the left to toggle between Logic 1 (HIGH) and Logic 0 (LOW).

• Output LEDs on the right illuminate bright red when driven HIGH (1) and stay dark when LOW (0).

4. Verification & Reference Circuit

• Click Lab Manual on the top right to open the Observation Book sidebar.

• Click Verify Circuit to have the virtual Lab Instructor check your wiring against all test vectors.

• Click Load Reference Circuit to view and study the textbook wiring.
